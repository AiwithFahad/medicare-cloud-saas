Everything in one place

Every department. One login.

Nine connected modules share the same patient record, so reception, doctors, pharmacy, lab and accounts always see the same truth.

Patient Management

Registration with patient codes, blood group, allergies and emergency contacts — searchable in seconds from anywhere in the hospital.

Appointments & Scheduling

OPD, follow-up, emergency and teleconsult booking with a clear status flow from scheduled to completed — no double bookings.

Doctors & Departments

Specializations, qualifications, consultation fees and weekly schedules for every doctor, organised by department.

Wards & Bed Management

A live bed board across General, ICU and Private wards — available, occupied, reserved and maintenance at a glance.

Pharmacy & Prescriptions

Medicine stock with reorder alerts and expiry tracking, plus prescriptions that flow straight from the doctor to dispensing.

Laboratory

Test catalogue with normal ranges, ordered results and a technician workflow from pending to completed reports.

Billing & Invoicing

Itemised patient invoices — consultation, lab, pharmacy, bed and procedure charges — with partial payments handled cleanly.

Reports & Analytics

Patient flow, department load, bed occupancy and revenue trends, charted automatically from live operational data.

Multi-tenant SaaS & Roles

Each hospital is fully isolated, with 9 role-based logins — from Super Admin to the patient portal — and subscriptions built in.

Nine built-in roles: Super Admin (platform), Hospital Admin, Doctor, Receptionist, Nurse, Accountant, Pharmacist, Lab Technician and Patient — each with its own permissions, so people only see the modules their job needs.

Paid plans start with a 14-day trial (30 days on Enterprise) — no card required. When you subscribe, use coupon WELCOME10 for 10% off your first payment. Payments are only marked verified after confirmation by our team, never automatically.
